What ClearToList is

ClearToList is a free screening aid. It compares your photos with official U.S. product recalls from the Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) to help you decide what to check more closely.

No guarantee

  • Results can be wrong in both directions. It can miss a recall, and it can suggest one that doesn't apply.
  • "No match found" means we found no match in CPSC recalls. It doesn't mean an item is safe.
  • Always confirm with the official recall notice at cpsc.gov/Recalls or with the manufacturer before you sell, buy or use an item.
  • Car seats, vehicles, food, drugs and cosmetics aren't covered.
