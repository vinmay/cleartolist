ClearToList

For resellers: photograph your thrift haul at home, then check each item against U.S. CPSC recalls before you list it. Built for children's products first: cribs, strollers, high chairs, bassinets, sleepers, play yards and toys.

Waking up the server. On free hosting the first request after a quiet period can take up to a minute.

Scan a photo

 

Your photos: JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 8 MB. They're re-encoded with all metadata removed (including GPS location), used for this one scan by a self-hosted GPU worker running open models, and deleted as soon as the scan finishes or after two minutes, whichever comes first. The text result is kept for ten minutes so this page can show it. We keep only anonymous counts, never photos.

Example scans

Two-photo examples first: the item photo gives a shortlist of the closest recalls, and the label photo settles it by model number. These are simulated from held-out CPSC photos, misses included. In the held-out test, the right recall was in the 3-recall shortlist for 67% of 150 children's-product photos; CONFIRMED only fires on a model-number or UPC match (details in the evaluation).

Screening aid only, not legal advice. "No match" means no match in CPSC recalls, not that the item is safe. Always confirm at cpsc.gov. Car seats are regulated by NHTSA and not covered: check nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Recall data: U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (saferproducts.gov API), refreshed nightly. Not affiliated with or endorsed by CPSC.

Open source: github.com/vinmay/cleartolist. You can also run the whole scanner on your own computer.
