ClearToList

For resellers: photograph your thrift haul at home, then check each item against U.S. CPSC recalls on your own computer before you list it. Built for children's products first: cribs, strollers, high chairs, bassinets, sleepers, play yards and toys.

This is the lightweight online demo. Full photo scanning (Gemma 4 vision + SigLIP 2) runs locally on your own computer; this page does not run any vision models. Here you can look up recalls by model number, UPC, brand or name, and browse scans that were run locally. Get the local app.

Screening aid only, not legal advice. "No match" means no match in CPSC recalls, not that the item is safe. Always confirm at cpsc.gov. Car seats are regulated by NHTSA and not covered: check nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Recall data: U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (saferproducts.gov API), refreshed nightly. Not affiliated with or endorsed by CPSC.
