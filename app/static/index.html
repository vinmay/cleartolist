ClearToList

Photograph your haul at home, then check each item against U.S. CPSC recalls before you list it. Runs on your own computer with open models.

Screening aid only, not legal advice. "No match found" means no match in CPSC recalls, not that the item is safe. Always confirm at cpsc.gov. Car seats are regulated by NHTSA and are not covered; check nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Recall data: U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission. ClearToList is not affiliated with or endorsed by CPSC.
